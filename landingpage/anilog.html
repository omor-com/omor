AniLog for Android

The distraction-free anime companion. Track episodes, catalog your personal library, and browse seasonal releases with zero intrusive ads and complete offline privacy.

Download APK / Release View on GitHub

Built for Focused Anime Fans

Engineered with Kotlin and modern Android Jetpack Compose.

Offline-First Storage

All your watch history and custom notes stay directly on your device. Fast and accessible even without internet.

Episode Counter

One-tap increment and decrement episode counters. Mark entire seasons as completed with a single swipe.

Material You & AMOLED

Adaptive dynamic theming matching Android 12+ wallpaper colors, plus a true deep-black OLED dark mode.
